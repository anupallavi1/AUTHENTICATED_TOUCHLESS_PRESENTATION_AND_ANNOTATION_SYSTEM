Registration tips
  • Face photo Upload a clear, front-facing photo with good lighting. Only one face, no sunglasses, mask or heavy filters. This photo is used to recognise you at sign-in.
  • Voice file Upload your audio with minimum 10 seconds duration. You should input an audio of you reading 2-3 sentences. Only your voice will be able to give voice commands.
  • Password Pick something you will remember; you need it every time you sign in.
  • Be patient Analysing your voice takes a few seconds after you press Create account.
After registering, switch to the Login tab: your webcam will be used to verify your face.
